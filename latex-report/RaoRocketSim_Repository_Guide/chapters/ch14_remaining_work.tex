\chapter{Remaining Work and Program Goals}\label{ch:remaining-work}

\lead{What the program is trying to achieve now, and what is still needed to get there.}

This chapter consolidates the open work recorded across the repository into one register. Its sources are the plans in \code{docs/program/}, the dated audits in \code{docs/audits/}, the subsystem documents in \code{docs/subsystems/}, the ``Known limitations'' and ``Remaining work'' sections of \code{README.md}, the knowledge-system reports in \code{knowledge/reports/}, the defect sections of Chapters~\ref{ch:s01-s02} to~\ref{ch:builds}, and two documents that are not stored in the repository:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rev.}: \emph{LREKit Consolidated Technical Review and Forward Program}, dated 7 October 2026 and examining commit \code{6c04d92}. Page numbers refer to its 65-page revision exported on 8 October, which records the owner's decisions of 7 October.
  \item \textbf{Plan}: \emph{From Engine Design to Experimental Feedback}, the component data and research plan of 3 November 2026 (17 pages, compiled at \code{output/pdf/LREKit_Component_Data_and_Research_Plan.pdf}). Its recommendations are also in \code{docs/program/COMPONENT_DATA_AND_MULTIFIDELITY_PLAN_2026-11-03.md}, whose status is PROPOSED with no executable code.
\end{itemize}
The register was compiled on 9 November 2026 against commit \code{d636d85} plus the uncommitted working tree. Items marked ``verified'' were re-checked in the code for this chapter. The same register, with the same identifiers and with links to every source document, is \code{docs/program/REMAINING_WORK_AND_GOALS.md}.

\caution{Nothing in this chapter is a hardware qualification record. Every contour is stamped \code{hardware_qualified = False}. The default physical-release report is BLOCKED, with all 17 external-evidence requirements of \code{raosim/release_readiness.py} missing (ten for the engine, two for the injector, two for the regenerative system and three for the pumps). Where a document and the code disagree, the code wins, and the register says so.}

\textbf{Priorities.} \textbf{P0} blocks trust in a result today: a crash without a record, a false ``feasible'', a silent substitution of physics, or a legal or release blocker. \textbf{P1} is near-term work inside the owner's first stage, credibility. \textbf{P2} is later work: after the credibility gate, on the research track, or blocked on literature or hardware.

\textbf{Identifiers.} Items are named \texttt{RW-}\emph{group}\texttt{-}\emph{nn}. The groups are the pipeline stages S01--S14 and the cross-cutting groups X01--X08 of Chapter~\ref{ch:cli-dataflow}, plus PRG for program-level release and governance work. An identifier is never reused; a closed item moves to Table~\ref{tab:rw-closed}.

\section{Validation and qualification path}\label{sec:rw-validation}

The review grades evidence in four levels (Rev.\ \S9.1 p.\,28). V0 is mathematical verification against an exact or manufactured solution. V1 is agreement with an independent implementation of the same equations. V2 is agreement with an independent physical measurement. V3 is system-level validation from several coupled outputs of one physical configuration. No LREKit model holds V2 evidence from its own hardware, and V3 waits for the M4 and M5 articles. CFD and FEA are computational evidence and never count as validation on their own (Rev.\ \S8.2 p.\,26).
