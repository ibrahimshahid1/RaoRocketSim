\chapter{Injector, Spray Coupling, Feed System and Electric Pumps}\label{ch:s08-s09}

\lead{Stages S08 and S09 of the traditional pipeline}

\stagetag{S08} sizes (or evaluates) a pintle injector from the cycle mass-flow
split, screens its hydraulics, spray, manifold, face/tip heating and stability,
and closes a per-propellant feed-pressure budget; optionally it closes a fixed
point between the spray vaporization screen and the cycle mass flow.
\stagetag{S09} consumes that budget and sizes an electric pump feed: centrifugal
impeller, inducer, diffuser/volute, motor, inverter and battery, each with
screening gates. Both run inside \code{raosim.run_nozzle.main} after the cooling
analysis (\stagetag{S07}) and before the bolted-interface and CAD stages
(\stagetag{S10}, \stagetag{S11}). They share one gate record,
\code{raosim.injector.InjectorGate} (status \texttt{pass}, \texttt{warn},
\texttt{fail} or \texttt{info}), and one hand-off object, the
\code{FeedSystemLedger}.

Abbreviations: TMR, total momentum ratio (radial over axial stream momentum);
$C_d$, discharge coefficient; SMD, Sauter mean diameter ($d_{32}$); NPSH, net
positive suction head (carried here as a pressure); $N_s$, $N_{ss}$,
non-dimensional specific and suction specific speed; BOM, bill of materials;
VOF, volume of fluid; MDO, multidisciplinary design optimization; DN, bearing
bore in millimetres times shaft speed in rpm.

\caution{Nothing in these two stages is physically validated. Every spray,
atomization, stability, thermal and pump-efficiency number is a screening
estimate. The injector always emits a \texttt{validation\_status} gate at
\texttt{warn} stating that cold-flow and hot-fire validation are required and
not performed (\code{raosim/injector.py:4349-4353}); pump CAD reports
\code{cold_flow_release_ready = False} and \code{hardware_qualified = False};
\code{summary["hardware_qualified"]} is always \code{False}.}

\section{S08 -- Pintle injector sizing and the optional spray/c-star fixed point}\label{sec:s08}

\subsection{Role in the data flow and call site}

The injector block is \code{if args.injector == "pintle":} at
\code{raosim/run_nozzle.py:5623}. It sits after cooling on purpose: the comment at
\code{:5618-5622} states that the fuel feed state ($T$, $P$, hence $\rho$, $\mu$,
$\sigma$) must come from the regenerative jacket outlet when the fuel is the
coolant. The block refuses to run without real propellant identities (return 2),
checks that every movable-pintle validity range is a min/max pair, and builds one
\code{InjectorSpec} (\code{:5701-5849}). Two closures do the work:
\code{evaluate_cli_cooling} (\code{:5871}) returns the S07 result unchanged without
\code{--regen} and otherwise re-runs Bartz heat flux and
\code{physics.regenerative_cooling_analysis} at a trial mass flow, and
\code{evaluate_cli_injector} (\code{:5903}) splits a total mass flow by O/F and
calls \code{injector.evaluate_pintle_injector}.

On the default path the CLI calls \code{evaluate_cli_injector(perf.m_dot, prop)}
once (\code{:6182}) and records the spray coupling as disabled (``vaporization
screen is report-only by default''). With \code{--spray-cstar-coupling} it calls
\code{spray_coupling.solve_spray_cstar_fixed_point} (\code{:5989}) with the
evaluator \code{spray_evaluator} (\code{:5959}), which rebuilds a trial propellant
with the iterate's $\eta_{c^*}$, re-solves cooling with the coolant flow set to the
new fuel flow, and re-evaluates the injector. After convergence it recomputes
\code{compute_engine_performance} (\code{:5998}), updates the mass flows on
\code{args}, and regenerates \code{cooling_profile.png}, \code{channel_march.png},
\code{cross_section.png} and \code{structural_life.png} from the final iterate
(\code{:6017-6156}).

\code{InjectorUnsupportedState}, \code{InjectorSpecError}, \code{RuntimeError} and
\code{ValueError} are caught (\code{:6188-6193}): \code{summary["injector"]}
records \code{feasible: false} with the message, \code{summary.json} is written,
and the run returns 2. After S09, failing injector gates block the chamber export
(return 2) unless \code{--allow-infeasible-injector} (\code{:6361-6365}).
\code{--throttle-map} is evaluated later in the S10/S11 block
(\code{injector.throttle_map}, called at \code{:6442}). Outside the CLI the same
boundary is called by \code{raosim/design.py:759} (\code{design_nozzle_v2}, used by
legacy \code{main.py} and the MDO re-evaluation), which also wires the spray
fixed point (\code{design.py:827}).

\subsection{What the header comments establish}

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/injector.py}: sizing principle and hydraulics}

The header's rule is that the injector ``is sized from the operating point the
nozzle + chamber solver already produces, never from an independent injector
thrust input'', along
$F,P_c,P_a,\varepsilon,\mathrm{O/F}\rightarrow C_F,A_t,\dot m\rightarrow\dot m_f,\dot m_o
\rightarrow A_f,A_o,\Delta p_f,\Delta p_o\rightarrow$ geometry and spray, with the
core relations attributed to Sutton and Biblarz (\emph{Rocket Propulsion
Elements}) and NASA SP-8089 (\emph{Liquid Rocket Engine Injectors}):
\begin{equation*}
\dot m = C_d A\sqrt{2\rho\,\Delta p},\quad v=\frac{\dot m}{\rho A},\quad
\mathrm{TMR}=\frac{\dot m_r v_r}{\dot m_a v_a},\quad
\mathrm{Re}=\frac{\rho v D_h}{\mu},\quad
\mathrm{We}=\frac{\rho v^2 D_h}{\sigma},\quad
\mathrm{Oh}=\frac{\mu}{\sqrt{\rho\sigma D_h}} .
\end{equation*}
Geometry is an axial annulus $A_a=\tfrac{\pi}{4}(D_o^2-D_i^2)$ and radial slots
$A_r=Nwh$; the code adds round holes ($d=\sqrt{4A_r/(N\pi)}$) and the Son gap.
Auto slots use $w=\sqrt{A_r/(N\,AR)}$, $h=AR\,w$, $D_h=2wh/(w+h)$,
web $\pi D_p/N-w$, blockage $Nw/(\pi D_p)$ and depth $(L/D)D_h$ with $L/D=2$
(\code{:1692}). Without a pintle diameter, $D_p=0.30D_c$ (\code{:2882}); the
model registry labels this a packaging heuristic (\code{assumption_only}) that
``must not be presented as a NASA or textbook sizing law''.

Gas and dense-gas streams use the compressible branch of \code{_stream_mass_flux}
(\code{:1753}), citing White, \emph{Fluid Mechanics} 7th ed., Eqs.~9.32 and
9.46--9.47, and warning that $P_0=P_c+\Delta p$ and the feed temperature are
treated as stagnation values and that real-gas accuracy is not established. With
$r=P_c/P_0$ the flow is choked for $r\le(2/(\gamma+1))^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)}$,
$G=C_dP_0\sqrt{\gamma/(RT_0)}\,(2/(\gamma+1))^{(\gamma+1)/(2(\gamma-1))}$, and
otherwise $G=C_dP_0\sqrt{\tfrac{2\gamma}{(\gamma-1)RT_0}(r^{2/\gamma}-r^{(\gamma+1)/\gamma})}$.
The movable radial sheet requires liquid; two-phase states and unsupported exit
geometries are rejected.

\textbf{Feed properties} (\code{resolve_feed_state}, \code{:1483}): explicit
overrides win; then CoolProp HEOS (Bell et al., \emph{Ind.\ Eng.\ Chem.\ Res.}\ 53,
2014) for oxygen, methane, hydrogen, ethanol, water and nitrous oxide (default
inlet 90, 112, 21, 298, 298, 270~K); then a constant-property table (Sutton and
Biblarz, NASA SP-8087, CRC) for RP-1/Jet-A, MMH, N$_2$O$_4$, UDMH and a LOX
fallback, ``screening-grade (no T-dependence modelled)''. \code{_classify_phase}
(\code{:1648}) sends $T\ge0.98T_{crit}$ and $P\le P_v$ to the compressible branch
and rejects $P<1.10P_v$ as a flashing state ``neither branch can size''.

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/injector.py}: momentum, spray and screening extras}

The header's maturity statement: SP-8089 names momentum balance, annulus/slot
geometry and deflector angle as the governing variables ``while warning that spray
distributions generally require cold-flow testing — so every spray/atomization
number below is a clearly-labeled screening surrogate, not a validated result''.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Spray direction.} $\theta=\operatorname{atan2}(M_r\cos\delta,\,M_a+M_r\sin\delta)$,
$M=\dot m v$; wall intercept $x_w=x_{imp}+(R_c-r_0)/\tan\theta$ (\code{:3236-3258}).
\item \textbf{Atomization} (\code{:1814}, \code{:1892}; model
\code{hinze_reitzbracco_d2law_liquid_only_screen_v2}). $\rho_g=P_c/(RT_c)$;
Hinze critical-Weber SMD (\emph{AIChE J.}\ 1, 1955),
$d_{32}=\min(13\sigma/(\rho_gv^2),D_h)$; breakup length $L_b=15D_h$ (midpoint of
the Reitz--Bracco range); $d^2$-law with $K=10^{-6}$\,m$^2$/s (``hydrocarbon
class''), $t_{99}=d_{32}^2(1-0.01^{2/3})/K$, $L_{vap}=vt_{99}$; vaporized fraction
$1-(d_{rem}^2/d_{32}^2)^{3/2}$ over residence $\max(0,L_c-L_b)/v$; poor regime
below $\mathrm{We}_g=40$. Only liquid, subcritical sheet/slot/hole streams
qualify; the screen is off for $P_c\ge P_{crit}$ and for the Son sheet. It returns
only $\eta_{vap}$ (liquid-mass-weighted); $\eta_{mix}$, $\eta_{comb}$, $\eta_{c^*}$
are \code{None}, and \code{predicted_cstar_efficiency} survives as a deprecated
alias that ``must not be interpreted as characteristic-velocity efficiency''.
\item \textbf{Manifold} (\code{:1948}): 1-D annular two-header square-law network
(NASA SP-8087; Kang and Sun, JTHT 25, 2011), ``requires 3-D manifold validation'';
its loss is reported as \code{manifold_screen_loss} and not charged to the budget.
\item \textbf{Face/tip thermal} (\code{:2018}): recovery $0.8T_c$, recirculation
velocity $0.2v$, Dittus--Boelter both sides, 1-D series circuit (``cf.\ the SP-125
regen wall solve''); unresolved material falls back to $k=350$\,W/m\,K,
$T_{lim}=800$\,K.
\item \textbf{Stability} (\code{:2095}; NASA SP-8113/SP-194, Crocco--Cheng):
chug on $\min\chi$, $\chi=\Delta p/P_c$ (good $\ge0.20$, prone $<0.10$);
$f_{L1}=a/(2L)$, $f_{T1}=1.8412a/(\pi D)$, $f_{R1}=3.8317a/(\pi D)$; $n$--$\tau$
band $0.1<\tau f_{L1}<0.5$ with $\tau$ the development time. A real assessment
``needs the feed admittance, the combustion response function, and damping''.
\item \textbf{Throttle map} (\code{:2565}): for \code{fixed_discrete} a
``commanded-effective-area study \ldots\ no physical stroke is claimed'' (registry:
\code{derived_schedule_not_hardware_model}); for the Son architecture the hardware
is sized once, the opening is solved per point, and a separate upstream
controller $\Delta p$ is solved for the fixed annulus. $P_c(f)=P_{c,full}f^{n}$,
$n$ from \code{--throttle-pc-exponent} (default 1).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/injector.py}: feed ledger and regenerative hand-off}

\code{FeedLineSpec} cites Huzel and Huang (NASA SP-125), Sutton and Biblarz, and
SP-8052 for NPSH, and states that unknown pump or tank data yield an
\texttt{info} requirement ``instead of judging a pump it was never told about''.
\code{feed_system_ledger} (\code{:3302}) computes per stream
\begin{gather*}
P_{req}=P_c+\Delta p_{inj}+\Delta p_{man}+\Delta p_{regen}+\Delta p_{line}+\Delta p_{ctrl},\\
\Delta p_{pump}=\max(0,P_{req}-P_{tank}),\qquad
H=\frac{\Delta p_{pump}}{\rho g_0},\qquad
\mathrm{NPSH}_a=P_{tank}-P_v ,
\end{gather*}
and an ``ideal'' pump power $Q\Delta p_{pump}/\eta_{pump}$; only the coolant stream
carries the jacket loss. \code{evaluate_pintle_injector} adds the regen coupling:
with the fuel as coolant, coolant plus explicit film flow must match the cycle fuel
flow within 1\% (pass), below 5\% (warn), else fail (\code{:866-867}). Only on a
pass are the jacket outlet $T$, $P$ handed to the fuel feed; a conflicting
explicit fuel inlet state fails the gate. A film bypass is a common upstream pump
then a split; ``Film-slot/orifice hardware and its branch-specific
pressure/thermal state remain outside this screening model''.

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/movable_pintle.py}}

The header cites Son et al., ``Design Procedure of a Movable Pintle Injector for
Liquid Rocket Engines'', JPP 33(4), 2017, DOI 10.2514/1.B36301, and keeps the
module ``intentionally separate'' from fixed slots and holes. Son Eq.~1 is
evaluated in the stable expanded form
\begin{equation*}
A_{tip}=\pi\left[2r_fL\cos\theta-L^2\sin\theta\cos^2\theta\right],\qquad
r_f=\tfrac12D_{post}-t_{post},\qquad
A_{cg}=\tfrac{\pi}{4}(D_{cg}^2-D_{pr}^2),
\end{equation*}
with limit $2\pi r_fL$ at $\theta\to0$. At $A_{tip}=A_{cg}$ ``pintle travel no
longer controls the minimum flow area'' (the paper reports an abrupt transition),
so the open stop defaults to $A_{tip}/A_{cg}=0.95$; an open stop at or beyond the transition
raises \code{ValueError} (an \code{InjectorSpecError} is a \code{ValueError} subclass; the CLI catches both). ``No universal discharge-coefficient-versus-stroke
or actuator-force law is invented'': a $C_d(L/L_{max})$ map is accepted only with a
source, 64-character SHA-256 and a fingerprint of schema
\code{raosim.son2017_movable_geometry.v1}; otherwise the constant $C_d$ is
labelled \code{constant_uncalibrated}. The static ledger is
$F_{req}=SF(F_p+F_m+F_{pre}+F_{fr}+F_{in})$, $F_p=\Delta p\,A_{unbal}$ (never
inferred), default $SF=1.5$. ``Dynamic response, seal life, thermal growth, and
qualification are outside this preliminary model''; \code{to_dict} hard-codes
\code{hardware_qualified: False}.

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/spray_coupling.py}}

The default path ``remains independent of the screening atomization correlation'';
only with user-supplied mixing and chemical-completion efficiencies does the
module close $\eta_{c^*}\rightarrow\dot m\rightarrow$ geometry $\rightarrow\eta_{vap}\rightarrow\eta_{c^*}$,
and it ``does not infer mixing or combustion efficiency and it refuses to use an
inapplicable gas/transcritical droplet result''. The iteration (\code{:210}) is
\begin{equation*}
\dot m_k=\frac{P_cA_t}{c^*_{id}\eta_k},\qquad
\eta_{raw}=\eta_{vap}(\dot m_k)\,\eta_{mix}\,\eta_{comb},\qquad
\eta_{k+1}=\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\eta_k+\omega(\eta_{raw}-\eta_k),\,\eta_{min},\,1\right),
\end{equation*}
with $\omega=0.5$, tolerance $10^{-4}$, 25 iterations and $\eta_{min}=0.40$
($\eta_{raw}<\eta_{min}$ raises). The \code{legacy_screen} source rejects any
inapplicable or out-of-regime stream instead of renormalizing; the
\code{lagrangian} source requires a typed \code{SprayCycleHandoff} with
\code{coupling_eligible} true. The evaluator runs once more at the reported state,
and convergence also requires closure within $\max(\mathrm{tol},2\mathrm{tol}/\omega)$.

\subsection{Role in the data flow and call site}

S09 runs only with \code{--electric-pump}, inside the injector block right after
\code{pintle.json}: \code{if args.electric_pump:} (\code{run_nozzle.py:6207}),
then \code{pumps.size_electric_pumps(inj.feed_system, _pump_spec_from_args(args))}
(\code{:6212}). \code{_validate_pump_args} (\code{:796}) requires
\code{--injector pintle}, switches the pump on for \code{--pump-visualize}, and
refuses \code{--pump-cad-format step|both} without CadQuery. A bare or
\code{--complete-package} run sets the pintle injector and the electric pump and
supplies tank pressures of $5\times10^5$~Pa (fuel) and $6\times10^5$~Pa (oxidizer)
(\code{:607-658}). The technology set is resolved at parse time:
\code{_apply_electric_technology} (\code{:522}, called at \code{:3792}) fills the
eight drive/battery flags from \code{get_electric_technology} and records the set
id, or \code{custom} when any of the eight was typed.

The S11 pump CAD hand-off (\code{pump_cad.export_pump_package},
\code{pump_cad_brep.export_pump_brep_package}) and the S12 pump GIF run inside the
same block (\code{:6239-6351}). A failed pump gate writes \code{summary.json} and
returns 2 unless \code{--allow-infeasible-pump} (\code{:6352-6357}). This check
precedes the injector check, so pump artifacts are written even when the injector
then blocks the run. The MDO re-evaluation calls \code{size_electric_pumps} from
\code{raosim/mdo/postprocess.py:1123}, inside \code{try}/\code{except}.

\subsection{Inputs and outputs}

Inputs are the \code{FeedSystemLedger} (per line: mass and volumetric flow,
density, viscosity, vapour pressure, required outlet pressure, rise, head, NPSH)
and a \code{PumpSizingSpec}: drive efficiencies, power densities, voltage, RPM
(\code{--pump-max-rpm} 120\,000) and optional power, current, torque and heat
limits; battery densities, efficiency, margin, voltage, current limit, vehicle
mass and mass-fraction limit; \code{--burn-time} (traditional default 10~s,
\code{:500}; the MDO keeps \code{MissionSpec.burn_time} = 120~s); optional pump
efficiencies (default solved); $\psi=0.55$, $\phi=0.08$, tip-speed limit 350~m/s
and 2500~m per stage. A line without tank pressure has no rise; it gets an
\texttt{info} gate and no geometry.

\textbf{Outputs.} \code{ElectricPumpSizingResult.to_dict()} goes to
\code{summary["electric_pump"]} and \code{pump.json}, its \code{hardware_bom} to
\code{pump_bom.json}, and per-line \code{reference_geometry} to
\code{pump_reference_geometry.json} (\code{:6217-6238}). Per line it holds the
duty, drive, impeller, inducer, diffuser/volute, meanline, curve, architecture,
system curve and thermal/stress ledger; globally the battery, gates, an
\code{assumptions} block (technology id, bus voltage) and the BOM and mass roll-up.

\subsection{What the header comments establish}

\subsubsection*{\code{raosim/pumps.py}}

The injector module ``already owns the feed-pressure balance''; this module adds
``first-pass electric drive, battery, centrifugal pump, inducer, diffuser/volute,
and feasibility screens'', all ``preliminary sizing rules anchored to the standard
rocket turbopump design variables \ldots\ not hardware qualification''.
\code{LITERATURE_SOURCES} names NASA SP-125 (two-feed-role BOM only), SP-8109
(meanline, $N_s$/$D_s$, diffuser/volute, tip speed, staging), SP-8052 (NPSH,
$N_{ss}$, inducer), Lee et al.\ 2021 (mass closure only, ``not as universal pump
RPM or pump efficiency defaults'') and Spiller, Stabile and Lentini 2013 (poor
efficiency of very small off-the-shelf pumps). The implemented chain:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Duty.} $Q=\dot m/\rho$, $P_{hyd}=\Delta p\,Q$, $P_{sh}=P_{hyd}/\eta_p$,
$P_{el}=P_{sh}/(\eta_m\eta_i)$, torque $P_{sh}/\omega$.
\item \textbf{Speed} (\code{_select_rpm}, \code{:1121}): target
$N_s=\omega\sqrt Q/(g_0H_{st})^{3/4}=0.45$ (clamped 0.10--1.20), bounded by
$D_2$ of 8--180~mm, $b_2\ge0.3$~mm, $b_2/D_2\le0.12$ and the RPM limit.
\item \textbf{Impeller} (\code{:1245}): $U_2=\sqrt{g_0H_{st}/\psi}$, $D_2=2U_2/\omega$,
$D_s=D_2(g_0H_{st})^{1/4}/\sqrt Q$, outlet blade angle fixed at $25^\circ$, stages
$\lceil H/2500\,\mathrm{m}\rceil$. Blade count from a digitized SP-8109 Fig.~16
(``zero prewhirl, SHROUDED impellers'', diameter ratio 0.65, read-off about
$\pm0.02$ in $\psi$), snapped to a multiple of the inducer count (SP-8052
\S3.1.14); four blades reach the eye, the rest start as splitters. Blockage
$B=Zt/(2\pi r\sin\beta)\le0.20$ inlet, $\le0.15$ exit; the root closure
$t\ge\sqrt{6F_{blade}/(\sigma_y/\mathrm{FoS})}$ feeds back into thickness,
diameter and RPM.
\item \textbf{Eye/shaft} (\code{:3547}): $Q=\pi(R_1^2-R_h^2)(1-B_1)\phi_1\omega R_1$,
with the hub enclosing the torsion-sized shaft, a 15~$\mu$m fit clearance and the
root wall; non-convergence raises \code{RuntimeError}.
\item \textbf{Meanline} (\code{:1406-1568}): Stodola-style slip
$\mathrm{clip}(1-\pi\sin\beta_2/Z,0.55,0.92)$, $c_{u2}=\sigma U_2-c_{m2}/\tan\beta_2$,
Euler head $U_2c_{u2}/g_0$, zero prewhirl and incidence. Losses are fixed fractions
of stage head (friction by Reynolds band, loading, incidence, disk, 2.5\% leakage,
recirculation by flow and $N_s$) with no per-coefficient citation;
$\eta=\mathrm{clip}(H_{st}/(H_{st}+\sum h),0.20,0.78)$, a cap above which values
``should come from a real pump curve''.
\item \textbf{Inducer} (\code{:2489}; SP-8052 \S\S2.1.9--3.1.15):
$N_{ss}=\omega\sqrt Q/(g_0\mathrm{NPSH})^{3/4}$; 3 blades, solidity 2.5, hub ratio
0.35, incidence ratio 0.425 (``a mean value, 0.425, has gained preference''), tip
angle $\arctan\phi_{tip}/(1-0.425)$, constant lead $r\tan\beta$, 0.005~in leading edge.
\item \textbf{Diffuser/volute} (\code{:2576}): volute if $N_s<0.20$, else 8 vanes;
throat $\max(1.15Q/c_{m2},0.35A_{exit})$; exit area $\max(1.8A_{th},Q/(0.35U_2))$
on $A(\theta)=A_{exit}\theta/2\pi$ (\code{constant_mean_velocity_screen}).
\item \textbf{Curve, drive, battery.} Synthetic curve at flow ratios 0.50--1.30
(\code{pump.synthetic_map}, \code{assumption_only}); one shared DC bus chosen from
24--800~V at a 250~A target; motor mass $P_{sh}/\delta_m$, inverter
$P_{sh}/(\eta_m\delta_{inv})$ (Lee Eqs.~26--29); battery
$m_b=\kappa_b\max(P_{el}t_b/(\eta_E\delta_E),P_{el}/\delta_P)$.
\item \textbf{Thermal/stress} (\code{:2608}): 70\% of pump loss heat plus disk
friction into the propellant (15~K limit); hoop $\rho U^2$; blade-root bending;
torsion $16T/(\pi d^3)$; casing hoop; DN limit $10^6$; seal speed 35~m/s; rotor
7800~kg/m$^3$, yield 600/300~MPa (rotor/casing), FoS 1.5.
\end{itemize}
The gates in \code{_feasibility} (\code{:3141}) screen bus voltage, missing tank
pressure (\texttt{info}), auto efficiency $\ge0.74$ (warn), power, RPM, torque,
current and heat limits, tip speed (warn at 0.85, fail at 1.0 of the limit), $N_s$
(pass 0.10--1.20), $D_s$ (pass 1.5--12), stages, free areas, eye continuity, shaft
fit, split-casing clamp and tool access, capacity, NPSH and $N_{ss}$ ($\le4$ pass),
system-curve margin, stresses, DN, seal speed and battery current, mass fraction
(warn above 20\%), power/energy balance and heat.

\code{software_verified_not_joint_qualified}; \code{pump.auto_efficiency_flow_head}
and \code{pump.synthetic_map} \code{assumption_only}; CAD is
\code{reference_geometry_not_hardware_qualified}.
\item \textbf{Fixed assumptions.} $\beta_2=25^\circ$, $\psi=0.55$, $\phi=0.08$, a
scalar tip-speed limit, efficiency cap 0.78, uncited loss constants,
constant-mean-velocity volute, rectangular diffuser vanes. In the archived 13~kN
build both pumps hit the 0.78 cap (warn) and the battery was power-limited
(0.38~kg energy branch vs 8.92~kg power branch before margin).
\item \textbf{Uncaught pump exceptions.} \code{size_electric_pumps} can raise
\code{ValueError}/\code{RuntimeError} (e.g.\ ``coupled shaft/hub envelope leaves no
credible impeller-eye annulus'', \code{pumps.py:2071}; \code{:3650}, \code{:3742}).
The call at \code{run_nozzle.py:6212} is outside the injector \code{try}, and
neither \code{raosim/cli.py} nor \code{lrekit/cli.py} catches exceptions, so the run
ends with a traceback and no \code{summary.json}. The pump audit reproduced this at
500~N; it was not re-run here. The MDO path wraps the same call.
\item \textbf{Bolt-layout fit not gated.} \code{bolt_layout_fits_clear_arc} is
computed (\code{pumps.py:1927}) but \code{split_casing_bolt_clamp_screen} and the
B-rep assembly gate read only the stress flag \code{bolt_screen_passed}
(\code{:1959}, \code{:3369}; \code{pump_cad_brep.py:915}). If the bolt-size ladder
runs out without a fit the design can still report feasible (the audit's 130~kN
finding); tests assert the fit only at 13~kN.
\item \textbf{Pump properties at the injector inlet.} The ledger takes $\rho$ and
$P_v$ from \code{result.feed[role]}, which for a closed direct-regen fuel path is
the jacket outlet state, although the pump is upstream of the jacket. RP-1 uses
constant properties, so nothing changes; a CoolProp coolant such as methane would
get pump flow and $\mathrm{NPSH}_a$ from heated properties. Code-reading
observation; no test covers it.
\item \textbf{Burn-time validity.} The 600~s warning fires only in
\code{MissionSpec}; \code{--electric-technology lee2021_ijass_electric_pump} with
the traditional 10~s burn applies the 600~s $\eta_E$ silently.
\item \textbf{Open items from the documents.} D1 machinability ceiling
($Z_2\lesssim28\sin\beta_2$), D2 process-dependent tip speed, D3
constant-moment-of-momentum volute and D4 flow-angle-matched diffuser vanes have no implementation
(grep of \code{pumps.py}, \code{pump_cad_brep.py}); D6 is partly covered by the root
closure. Bearings, seals, shaft retention, rotordynamics, measured pump and motor
maps, battery pulse data and cavitation tests are out of scope; propellant heating
is not fed back into properties or NPSH.
\end{itemize}
