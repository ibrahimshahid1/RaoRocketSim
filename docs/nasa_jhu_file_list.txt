Provenance note
---------------
The checked-in M3.5Perf outputs are public NASA sample outputs, but the exact
executable/source variant that generated `TT'.out` is not preserved in the
local tree or upstream history. See `docs/nasa_tt_prime_provenance.md`.
These files are historical fixture overlays. They are not promotion authority
for `NASA_REFERENCE_MATCHED`; the canonical track is the visible
`MOC_GridCalc_BDE.cpp` source-port workflow.

Files and parser entry points
-----------------------------
wall.out
  Final cropped wall contour. Columns: i, j, X/R*, R/R*, mach, theta(deg),
  pressure(psia). Parser: raosim.legacy_io.parse_wall_out.

center.out
  Final centerline trace. Columns: J, X/R*, R/R*, Mach, Pres, Temp, Rho,
  Theta, Gamma, MassFlow. Parser: parse_center_out.

rao.dat
  TDK/Rao wall table from ThetaB to exit. Columns: R/R*, X/R*, theta(deg).
  Parser: parse_rao_dat.

summary.out
  Human summary report with scalar fields, initial throat table, DE report,
  wall contour table, nozzle summary, and exit-plane data. Parser:
  parse_summary_out for scalar fields plus the initial throat table.

MOC_Grid.plt
  Tecplot multi-zone RRC contour grid. Variables: X/R, R/R, Mach, Theta,
  Massflow, I. One zone per J index. Parser: parse_moc_grid_plt.

MOC_SL.plt
  Tecplot multi-zone streamline output. Variables: X/R, Y/R, Z/R, R/R,
  Mach, Pres, Temp, Rho, Theta, Gamma, Massflow, J. Parser:
  parse_moc_sl_plt.

Summary.plt
  Tecplot primary contours. Zones: Wall contour, RRC BD, LRC DE. Variables:
  X/R, R/R, Mach, Pres, Temp, Rho, Theta, Gamma. Parser: parse_summary_plt.

ThetaB.out
  ThetaB secant iteration trace. Columns: I, ThetaB, xArcMax, LastKernelJ,
  paramErr. Parser: parse_theta_b_out.

axis_i.out
  Initial-kernel axis trace before final contour crop. Parser:
  parse_axis_i_out.

wall_i.out
  Initial-kernel wall trace before final contour crop. Parser:
  parse_wall_i_out.

TT'.out
  Initial throat line TT'. Columns: i, X, R, MACH, THETA, massflow. Parser:
  parse_tt_prime_out.

TT'BF_Kernel.out
  Kernel region from TT' through D-side split. Columns: i, j, x, r, mach,
  theta, press, temp. Parser: parse_kernel_out.

BFE_Kernel.out
  Remaining BFE region after point D. Columns: i, j, x, r, mach, theta,
  press, temp. Parser: parse_kernel_out.

LastKernel.out
  Final kernel RRC at the converged ThetaB. Columns: i, j, x, r, mach, theta,
  press, temp, Massflow. Parser: parse_last_kernel_out.

UncroppedKernel.out
  Full kernel before final crop. Columns: i, j, x(in), r(in), mach, theta,
  pressure(psi), temperature(R), density(slug/ft3), massflow. Parser:
  parse_uncropped_kernel_out.
