The original [Lee electric-pump paper](/Users/ibrahimshahid/Documents/Github/RaoRocketSim/propulsion_texts/10.1007_s42405-020-00325-z.pdf), DOI `10.1007/s42405-020-00325-z`, SHA-256 `00d2425ed35f3fe39b8debbfa852cdeb9a6f0ae006eca0296d9523ae7a499500`, defines inverter power density from **inverter output power** on PDF p. 7, Eqs. 28–29:

```text
delta_P,inv = P_inv,out / m_inv
m_inv = P_m,out / (eta_m * delta_P,inv)
P_inv,in = P_inv,out / eta_inv
```

The traditional and two MDO mass consumers used DC input with the same density parameter, adding an extra `1/eta_inv` to inverter mass. The repair changes separate inverter mass to use motor electrical input/inverter AC output, while retaining inverter-loss dependence in DC demand, current, battery sizing and heat. CLI/schema help, exported assumptions and BOM definitions now name the denominator. No numeric efficiency or density default was changed.

The legacy combined drive-density override retains its documented DC-input package convention and allocation, avoiding a silent reinterpretation of that separate compatibility path. The change spans [_drive_sizing](/Users/ibrahimshahid/Documents/Github/RaoRocketSim/raosim/pumps.py), [electric_feed](/Users/ibrahimshahid/Documents/Github/RaoRocketSim/raosim/mdo/pump.py) and [assembly readouts](/Users/ibrahimshahid/Documents/Github/RaoRocketSim/raosim/mdo/assembly.py). Freezing both chemistry and vibration is the source's distinct constant-heat-capacity limit. For the thermally perfect model `h=h(T), e=e(T)`, Anderson explicitly retains `a_f=sqrt(gamma*R*T)`. Reacting-mixture heat capacity additionally contains terms such as `sum[h_i*(partial c_i/partial T)_p]`; these terms must not be included in a fixed-composition heat-capacity grid.

The existing [frozen-flow implementation](/Users/ibrahimshahid/Documents/Github/RaoRocketSim/raosim/frozen_flow.py) is consistent with that thermally perfect fixed-composition framework. Its governing energy, continuity and sound-speed relations have implementation links; species-mixture construction, reaction terms and equilibrium sound speed remain comparison citations. The class consumes a supplied mixture grid rather than calculating its species sum. Numerical results were preserved. Module/registry documentation and result applicability now disclose single-temperature thermal properties and the absence of a separate vibrational temperature/relaxation equation.

Overall source support remains partial. The inspected pages do not establish the piecewise-linear property approximation, bisection policy, property-grid generation accuracy, independent thermochemistry agreement or the separate NASA SP-125 attribution. A composition string, generator description and artifact hash cannot prove that a grid was generated at fixed composition with the intended internal-energy treatment. No particular held grid was demonstrated wrong by this review; input-property scientific validation remains an explicit gap.
